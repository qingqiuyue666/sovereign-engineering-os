# No Fake Traction Policy

## Purpose

Keep validation records truthful while outreach and discovery are still in
progress.

## Rules

- Do not count repository files as market proof.
- Do not count internal confidence as demand.
- Do not count a friendly meeting as commitment.
- Do not count unpaid advice as revenue.
- Do not count a draft service page as customer adoption.
- Do not count a proposed price as validated pricing.
- Do not count one accepted delivery as product readiness.
- Do not publish client-identifying proof without approval.

## Required Labels

Use these labels until evidence closes the gap:

- `EVIDENCE_PENDING`
- `MARKET_PROOF_PENDING`
- `REAL_DELIVERY_PENDING`
- `HUMAN_ACTION_REQUIRED`

## Escalation

If a claim sounds stronger than the evidence:

1. downgrade the wording
2. attach the evidence record
3. mark the gap
4. request human approval if external wording is involved

## Boundary

This policy protects the wedge. It does not prevent outreach, discovery,
sales, or delivery. It prevents unsupported claims about those activities.
