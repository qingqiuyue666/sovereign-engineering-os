# Model Capability Matrix

## Purpose

Track which brain tier may perform which class of work.

## Scope

Covers strategic, engineering, audit, production, worker, and local/private
model use.

## Non-goals

- This is not a provider ranking.
- This does not enable live provider execution.
- This does not certify any model.

## Matrix

| Work Type | Strategic Brain | Engineering Brain | Auditor Brain | Production Brain | Worker Brain | Human Sovereign |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Market selection | Draft/reason | Advise feasibility | Challenge | Prepare material | No final role | Final |
| Architecture | Draft principles | Design | Review | Prepare diagrams | Low-risk edits only | Final for material risk |
| Code/docs execution | Scope | Execute | Review | Draft | Bounded tasks | Approve high-risk |
| Evidence claims | Propose | Attach evidence | Verify | Format | Collect only | Final external claim |
| High-risk operations | No approval | No approval | No approval | No approval | Forbidden | Required |
| Completion certification | No self-certify | No self-certify | Verify evidence | No final role | Forbidden | Final |

## Operating Rules

- Executor and auditor separation is mandatory for material risk.
- Worker brains can collect, format, and transform bounded material only.
- Human approval is required for external claims and high-risk operations.

## Failure Modes

- A model self-certifies its own work.
- Worker output becomes strategy.
- Provider capability is confused with authority.

## Upgrade Path

Bind matrix rows to task metadata after the first standardized delivery.
