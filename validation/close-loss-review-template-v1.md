## Purpose

Capture accepted, rejected, no-decision, unsafe, and wrong-segment outcomes
without turning them into false proof.

## Record Format

```text
date:
target identifier:
outcome: accepted / rejected / no decision / unsafe / wrong segment
reason:
evidence obtained:
what remains unknown:
what to change next:
whether battlefield remains valid:
limitations:
reviewer:
```

## Outcome Rules

### Accepted

Accepted means the target agrees to a concrete next step with owner, date,
scope, and evidence access.

It does not mean delivery is complete.

### Rejected

Rejected means the target declines after a specific offer, scope, price,
risk boundary, or evidence request.

Record the reason and whether it is target-specific or battlefield-level.

### No Decision

No decision means the target neither accepts nor rejects and no next step is
owned.

Do not count no-decision as positive signal.

### Unsafe

Unsafe means the target asks for work outside the SEIS boundary, such as
secret handling, account-rule bypass, uncontrolled automation, or external
promises without review.

Reject or rescope. Do not deliver unsafe work.

### Wrong Segment

Wrong segment means the target lacks workflow ownership, budget influence,
or relevant pain.

Record whether a referral exists.

## Change Rules

Update the battlefield only after patterns appear.

One rejection may change outreach copy. Repeated similar rejection may change
target profile, offer scope, pricing, or battlefield selection.

No outcome here is revenue, ROI, adoption, certification, or completed
delivery unless separate evidence proves it.
