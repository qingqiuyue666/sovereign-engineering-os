# Next Actions

## Purpose

Convert this assembly into immediate execution without overbuilding runtime
or overstating evidence.

## Scope

Covers the first 90 days after assembly.

## Non-goals

- Do not build protocols, credit, clearing, marketplace, or capital systems
  before paid delivery evidence.
- Do not add live model/provider execution by default.
- Do not claim external adoption until verified.

## 0-14 Days

- Build a target list of 25 teams likely to have AI-written code entering
  production.
- Create one service page from `distribution/service-page-template.md`.
- Send discovery using `transaction/discovery-script.md`.
- Reject prospects that seek uncontrolled automation or secret handling.
- Update `market/buyer-map.md` and `market/buying-trigger-map.md` from real
  responses.

## 15-45 Days

- Close one paid or equivalent high-commitment readiness audit.
- Deliver the audit using `trusted-delivery/delivery-sop.md`.
- Produce evidence package, acceptance record, failure record, and proof
  assets.
- Record objections, pricing, buyer trigger, and ROI hypothesis in
  `assets/`.

## 46-90 Days

- Decide whether to standardize, revise, or kill the first wedge.
- If repeated signal exists, create the first delivery standard candidate.
- If signal is weak, score Wedge B and Wedge D with updated evidence.
- Do not promote maturity stage claims beyond evidence.

## Operating Rules

- Every action must update a registry or report.
- Every claim must have an evidence link.
- Every delivery must produce proof and distribution material.
- Every risky operation must follow trusted-delivery and brain-governance
  rules.

## Failure Modes

- Research replaces buyer contact.
- Outreach sells generic AI automation rather than governance.
- Delivery produces a report but no reusable asset.
- The wedge is kept alive despite weak paid signal.

## Upgrade Path

After two accepted deliveries, create a versioned delivery standard and
stage-claim ledger.
