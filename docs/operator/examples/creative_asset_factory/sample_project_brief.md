# Sample Project Brief

## Goal

Create a planning-only brief for an 8-12 second VFX / AI / 3D hybrid sample with a clear beginning, one strong hero frame, and a disciplined finish.

## Creative Intent

- The piece begins with controlled field emergence.
- The piece resolves into one strong hero frame.
- The piece uses one motivated transition to move from field emergence into the hero frame and then into a clean resolve.
- The piece must feel commercially usable rather than experimental drift.

## Blocked Capabilities

- No provider execution.
- No production autonomy.
- No subprocess execution.
- No external tool launch.
- No prompt-only generation.

## Deliverable

Planning documents, review checklists, and rejection rules only.
