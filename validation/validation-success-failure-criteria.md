# Validation Success Failure Criteria

Status label: `VALIDATION_CRITERIA_READY`

Repository status: `SEIS_REAL_WORLD_VALIDATION_READY`

Real-world status: `HUMAN_ACTION_REQUIRED`

## Purpose

Define what real-world validation evidence means before any SEIS status is
upgraded.

Empty templates, internal enthusiasm, strategy documents, and generated
examples are not validation evidence.

## Weak Signal

Weak signal means:

- a real target responds with relevant interest
- the target confirms the workflow category exists
- the target asks a specific question about scope, risk, or price
- the target refers a more relevant person

Weak signal can update:

- outreach copy
- target profile
- objection list

Weak signal cannot claim:

- market proof
- paid signal
- buyer commitment
- delivery readiness

## Medium Signal

Medium signal means:

- a real discovery conversation occurs
- the target describes a repeated workflow
- pain, frequency, current tools, and approval points are recorded
- budget path or decision owner is at least partially identified
- evidence access is possible but not yet approved

Medium signal can update:

- buyer/budget/trigger score
- workflow examples
- scope language
- pricing hypothesis

Medium signal cannot claim:

- paid customer
- completed delivery
- ROI
- product-market fit

## Strong Signal

Strong signal means at least one of:

- a real buyer accepts paid scope
- a real buyer approves a paid pilot
- a high-commitment equivalent is recorded with owner, timeline, scope, and
  evidence access
- a real operator agrees to provide workflow evidence and review output

Strong signal can update:

- validation status for the target
- first bounded delivery readiness
- pricing history as real feedback
- delivery package selection

Strong signal still cannot claim:

- delivery completion before work is done
- case study before acceptance
- repeatability before repeated examples
- SaaS or product readiness

## Rejection

Rejection means the target declines after a specific message, scope, price,
trust boundary, or evidence request.

Rejection evidence should record:

- reason
- target segment
- offer version
- price if discussed
- objection
- whether the battlefield remains plausible

Rejection can improve the system. It is not failure unless repeated patterns
show the battlefield is wrong.

## No-Decision

No-decision means the target does not accept, reject, refer, or commit to a
next step.

No-decision should record:

- whether the trigger was weak
- whether the buyer was wrong
- whether the scope was unclear
- whether price was premature
- whether the message was too broad

## Invalid Battlefield

The battlefield should be considered invalid or paused if:

- 10 relevant targets produce no workflow-specific responses
- discovery repeatedly finds no urgent pain
- targets cannot identify a budget path
- evidence access is consistently blocked
- buyers only want unsafe full automation
- the workflow cannot be bounded without unacceptable risk
- price acceptance is absent after multiple qualified conversations

## What Is Not Evidence

The following are not validation evidence:

- repository files
- generated personas
- fake company names
- fake testimonials
- generic praise
- social likes
- "AI is interesting"
- a friendly meeting with no next step
- an internal opinion
- a draft service page
- an empty template

## Required Before Status Upgrade

Before any real-world status upgrade, record:

- source
- date
- actor or privacy-safe identifier
- workflow
- claim supported
- limitation
- evidence quality
- next action
- reviewer decision

If these fields are missing, keep the status as pending.
