# Discovery Script

## Purpose

Gather buyer, budget, trigger, trust, evidence, and acceptance data.

## Scope

Use for first-wedge discovery and adapt for other wedges.

## Non-goals

- Do not pitch generic AI automation.
- Do not request secrets.
- Do not promise certification or production authority.

## Questions

1. Where is AI-generated or AI-assisted work entering production today?
2. Who owns the risk if it fails?
3. What event would force you to improve governance this quarter?
4. What evidence would make leadership trust the process?
5. What evidence is currently missing?
6. What budget or avoided loss would fund fixing it?
7. What would make this unacceptable or too risky?
8. What would count as a useful first deliverable?
9. Who must review or approve the findings?
10. What happens if this remains unchanged for 90 days?

## Operating Rules

- End each call with trigger, budget, risk owner, and next proof step.
- Record objections in `assets/objection-library.md`.
- Reject if the buyer seeks hidden automation or secret exposure.

## Failure Modes

- Discovery becomes consulting without a transaction path.
- Buyer has no authority.
- Trigger is too weak.

## Upgrade Path

Convert repeated questions and answers into a standard intake form.
