# Close Loss Review

## Purpose

Review wins, losses, no-decisions, deferrals, and SEIS rejections so the
wedge improves from real outcomes.

## Review Table

| Date | Prospect | Outcome | Buyer | Trigger | Price | Primary Reason | Evidence Link | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- |
|  |  | won / lost / no decision / deferred / rejected |  |  |  |  |  | keep / revise / kill |

## Outcome Definitions

- `won`: paid or equivalent high-commitment scope accepted.
- `lost`: fit existed, offer was discussed, buyer declined.
- `no decision`: no clear next action.
- `deferred`: trigger may exist later.
- `rejected`: SEIS rejected unsafe or wrong-fit work.

## Review Questions

- Was the buyer correct?
- Was the trigger real?
- Was the budget path real?
- Was evidence access possible?
- Was the price wrong or was value unclear?
- Did rejection rules protect the system?
- What should change before the next conversation?

## Decision Rules

- Continue if triggers, budget paths, and next steps are visible.
- Revise if objections repeat around scope, trust, or pricing.
- Kill or rescore if the first 10 conversations show no buyer urgency,
  evidence access, or budget path.

## Boundary

Close/loss learning is not market proof. It is evidence for changing the
offer and for deciding whether to keep testing.
