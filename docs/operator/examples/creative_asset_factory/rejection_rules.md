# Rejection Rules

Reject the sample plan if any of the following are true:

- The plan drops the 8-12 second target.
- The plan loses the motivated transition.
- The plan loses the hero frame.
- The plan allows prompt-only generation.
- The plan implies Houdini, ComfyUI, DaVinci, AE, Blender, or Unreal execution.
- The plan lacks artifact control language.
- The plan lacks commercial usability criteria.
