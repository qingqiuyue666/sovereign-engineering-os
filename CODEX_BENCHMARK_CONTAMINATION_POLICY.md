# Codex Benchmark Contamination Policy

## Gate

Benchmarks are intake and criteria sources until a specific allowed subset is
selected, license and contamination risk are reviewed, execution rules are
recorded, and results are analyzed.

## Evidence Path

- `reports/checkpoints/global-source-freshness-audit-v1.md`
- `reports/checkpoints/frontier-gap-search-audit-v1.md`
- `reports/checkpoints/real-delivery-benchmark-sample-plan-v1.md`

## Non-Claim Boundary

External benchmark maturity is `UNPROVEN` unless a benchmark subset is actually
run with valid evidence.
