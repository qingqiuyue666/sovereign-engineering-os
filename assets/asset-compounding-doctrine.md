# Asset Compounding Doctrine

## Purpose

Make every validated delivery increase future leverage through reusable
cases, failures, templates, SOPs, benchmarks, objections, pricing data, and
standardization candidates.

## Core Rule

A delivery is not finished until at least one reusable asset decision is
made: create, update, reject, or defer.

## Compounding Ladder

| Level | Evidence Required | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Delivery record | Accepted or incomplete delivery package. | Delivery trace and handoff. |
| Case or failure | Buyer-approved case material or recorded failure. | Case library or failure library entry. |
| Template | Repeated structure or reusable artifact. | Template registry and reuse ledger entry. |
| SOP | Template reused successfully across comparable work. | SOP candidate with conditions. |
| Standard | SOP accepted across repeated deliveries and reviewed. | Standardization candidate. |
| Certification/protocol/credit support | Evidence across repeated standards and outcomes. | Readiness gate input, not automatic claim. |

## Non-Claims

No empty asset library, case library, benchmark, ROI, certification,
protocol, or credit claim may be made without records. Scaffold files are
`EXECUTION_KIT_READY`; real asset proof remains `EVIDENCE_PENDING` until
delivery data exists.

## Required Asset Decisions

After each delivery, decide whether to update:

- `assets/case-library.md`
- `assets/failure-library.md`
- `assets/customer-problem-library.md`
- `assets/objection-library.md`
- `assets/pricing-history.md`
- `assets/benchmark-history.md`
- `assets/roi-history.md`
- `assets/tool-reliability-records.md`
- `assets/template-reuse-ledger.md`
- `assets/standardization-candidate-ledger.md`

## Upgrade Path

Repeated patterns become SOPs. Repeated accepted SOPs can become standards.
Only repeated standards with external evidence may later support
certification, protocol, credit, rights, clearing, or capital-allocation
language.
