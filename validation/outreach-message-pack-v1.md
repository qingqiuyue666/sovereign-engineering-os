## Outreach Rules

- Messages must be sent manually by a human.
- Do not scrape contacts.
- Do not invent replies.
- Do not promise ROI, certification, account safety, or full automation.
- Do not request secrets or credentials.
- Position the offer as one bounded workflow with human confirmation.

## Direct Short Message

I am testing a small service for operators who have one repetitive workflow
that is wasting time or causing errors.

The scope is narrow: map the workflow, reduce the repeated prep work, add a
human review checkpoint, and leave behind an SOP, log, and failure rule. No
automatic payments, account changes, or external messages without approval.

Is there one workflow you would be willing to compare against this?

## Warm Intro Message

I am looking for operators who have a repeated admin, reporting, content, or
customer-data workflow that is annoying enough to fix but too risky to fully
automate.

that?
