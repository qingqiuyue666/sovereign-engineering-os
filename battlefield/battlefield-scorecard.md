# Battlefield Scorecard

## Purpose

Maintain candidate wedge scores with explicit assumptions.

## Scope

Scores are provisional and must be updated after discovery.

## Non-goals

- Scores are not proof.
- Scores do not replace buyer conversations.
- Scores do not claim market capture.

## Operating Rules

- Treat all scores as assumptions until discovery.
- A score below 70 is not first-wedge eligible.
- Update score reasons in the wedge file after each market contact.

## Failure Modes

- Optimism replaces evidence.
- Commodity risk is understated.
- Stage 10-16 path is asserted without a delivery standard.

## Upgrade Path

Add versioned score revisions after first 10 discovery contacts.
