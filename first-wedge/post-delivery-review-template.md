# Post-Delivery Review Template

## Purpose

Turn each delivery into learning for the wedge, templates, assets, pricing,
proof, and future standards.

## Review Questions

### Buyer And Trigger

- Was the buyer profile correct?
- Was the trigger strong enough?
- Did the budget source match the hypothesis?
- What objections appeared?

### Delivery

- Did scope stay bounded?
- Were evidence access and approval sufficient?
- Which review gates caught real issues?
- Which gates were missing or too heavy?
- Did executor/auditor separation matter?

### Proof And Assets

- What proof assets can be created safely?
- What must remain internal?
- What template should be updated?
- What failure should become a rule?
- What repeated pattern could become an SOP?

### Commercial

- Was pricing accepted, resisted, discounted, or rejected?
- What value did the buyer actually care about?
- What should change on the service page?

## Boundary

Do not turn one accepted delivery into market proof for the entire wedge.
Repeated evidence is required before standards, certification, product, or
protocol claims.
