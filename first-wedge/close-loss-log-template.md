## Outcome Definitions

- `won`: buyer accepts paid or equivalent high-commitment scope.
- `lost`: buyer rejects after clear fit, scope, and price discussion.
- `no decision`: buyer does not commit and no active next step exists.
- `deferred`: buyer has potential trigger but not now.
- `rejected`: SEIS rejects due to unsafe or wrong-fit requirements.

## Required Discipline

- Record no-decision separately from loss.
- Record unpaid interest separately from paid signal.
- Do not treat friendly feedback as market proof.
- Update pricing, objections, service page, and rejection rules from repeated
  patterns only.
