# First 10 Conversation Plan

## Purpose

Run a small validation sequence that can test the first wedge quickly without
pretending the market has already responded.

## Target Mix

| Conversation | Target |
| --- | --- |
| 1-3 | CTOs or founders with active AI-assisted engineering usage. |
| 4-5 | VP/Head of Engineering or platform leaders. |
| 6-7 | Security or compliance partners attached to engineering. |
| 8 | Staff engineer or release owner with workflow detail. |
| 9 | Buyer adjacent to an audit/customer-review trigger. |
| 10 | Deliberate wrong-fit prospect to test rejection rules. |

## Required Questions

- What AI-assisted engineering workflow exists now?
- Who owns the risk?
- What budget could fund governance?
- What trigger makes this urgent?
- What evidence can be shared?
- What would count as accepted audit output?
- What price range feels plausible or implausible?
- What would make this a no?

## Success After 10

The plan succeeds only if it produces useful evidence:

- at least 10 logged outcomes
- at least 3 clear trigger records
- at least 3 budget-source records
- at least 5 objections
- at least 3 pricing responses
- at least 1 qualified next step or a clear kill/revise decision

## Decision After 10

Choose one:

- continue the wedge
- narrow the segment
- change the trigger
- change the offer
- change pricing
- kill the wedge and rescore alternatives

Do not claim market proof from this plan unless paid-signal criteria are met.
