# Productization Roadmap

## Purpose

Describe the evidence-gated path from readiness audit service to possible
product forms.

## Roadmap

| Stage | Product Form | Evidence Required | Current Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Service package | Offer, delivery loop, validation kit. | `EXECUTION_KIT_READY` |
| 2 | Internal workbench | Repeated internal operator need or one accepted delivery loop. | `APP_SPEC_READY` |
| 3 | Template pack | Reused templates with acceptance records. | `EVIDENCE_PENDING` |
| 4 | Private deployment | Repeated buyer requests for internal tool use and support model. | `EVIDENCE_PENDING` |
| 5 | SaaS | Repeated self-serve demand, support/security readiness, retention signal. | `MARKET_PROOF_PENDING` |
| 6 | Marketplace/certification/protocol | Repeated standards and external trust demand. | `EVIDENCE_PENDING` |
| 7 | Credit/clearing/capital | Contribution tracking and economic control proof. | `EVIDENCE_PENDING` |

## Rule

Service remains service until repeated delivery patterns make a narrower
product cheaper, safer, and more valuable than custom delivery.
