# Outreach Message Bank

## Purpose

Provide bounded outreach messages for first validation conversations without
claiming traction, certification, or guaranteed ROI.

## Targeted Engineering Leader

Subject: AI-assisted code governance before production risk compounds

Message:

You may already have AI-assisted code showing up in PRs, tests, refactors,
or release work. I am packaging a bounded AI Production Governance Readiness
Audit for teams that need to prove how AI-assisted engineering work is
admitted, reviewed, tested, rolled back, and accepted.

The audit is not a tool rollout or certification claim. It maps one real
workflow, identifies evidence gaps, and gives you a prioritized governance
plan. Would it be useful to compare this against your current AI-code path?

## Founder / CTO

Subject: Quick check on AI-code production controls

Message:

If AI-assisted engineering is already part of your team, the next risk is
usually not model quality alone. It is whether the company can show who owns
AI-code risk, what evidence is required, and what happens when a change fails.

I am validating a 5-10 day readiness audit for one bounded workflow. The
output is a scorecard, evidence matrix, risk-tier map, and remediation plan.
Would a short discovery call be worth testing against your current process?

## Security Or Compliance Partner

Subject: Evidence trail for AI-assisted engineering work

Message:

Many teams have AI-assisted engineering activity before they have a clean
answer for audit trail, human approval, rollback, and external claims. I am
validating a bounded readiness audit that maps one workflow and marks what is
evidenced versus `EVIDENCE_PENDING`.

This is not compliance certification. It is a practical evidence and control
review for engineering leaders. Is there a current AI-code workflow where
that would be useful?

## Warm Referral Ask

Message:

I am looking for engineering teams where AI-assisted code is already entering
real development workflows and leadership wants clearer production
governance. Best-fit contacts are CTOs, Heads of Engineering, platform leads,
or security partners with a near-term release, customer review, audit, or AI
policy deadline.

Is there one person you would be comfortable introducing me to for discovery?

## Follow-Up After No Reply

Message:

I will close the loop here. The specific problem I am testing is whether
teams using AI-assisted engineering need a bounded audit of code admission,
review gates, evidence, rollback, and ownership before expanding usage.

If that is not current for you, no action needed. If it becomes relevant
before a release, customer review, audit, or AI policy rollout, I would be
glad to compare notes.
