# Paid Signal Criteria

## Purpose

Define what is strong enough to update the first wedge from hypothesis to
real paid signal.

## Counts As Paid Signal

| Signal | Required Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Paid audit | Signed scope, payment record or invoice, named buyer, timeline, acceptance criteria. |
| Paid pilot | Pilot terms, price, owner, workflow, evidence access, success criteria. |
| Written paid approval | Written approval from budget controller with price, scope, and start condition. |
| Equivalent high-commitment signal | Named executive buyer, committed timeline, approved evidence access, nontrivial internal resource allocation. |

## Does Not Count

- verbal interest without next step
- unpaid discovery call
- free diagnostic
- request for a proposal with no budget owner
- "circle back later"
- internal belief that someone would pay
- repository readiness
- social reaction or content engagement

## Minimum Record

Every paid-signal claim must include:

- buyer role
- budget source
- price or equivalent commitment
- date
- trigger
- scoped workflow
- evidence access
- acceptance criteria
- approval artifact or written note
- limitations

## Status Upgrade Rule

Do not change real-world status from `MARKET_PROOF_PENDING` until the record
exists. Do not claim revenue unless payment evidence exists.
