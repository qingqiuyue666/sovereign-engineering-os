# Service Page Draft

## Page Title

AI Production Governance Readiness Audit

## Subtitle

Evidence-backed review for teams putting AI-assisted engineering work into
real production workflows.

## Who It Is For

This audit is for engineering leaders, founders, platform owners, and
security/compliance partners who need to answer:

- Where is AI-assisted code entering production?
- What evidence proves it was reviewed, tested, approved, and reversible?
- Who owns the risk?
- What gaps must be closed before broader AI coding adoption?

## Problem

AI-assisted development can increase output faster than a team can inspect,
accept, and explain it. Without a governance layer, leaders may face unclear
ownership, inconsistent review gates, weak rollback evidence, and risky
external claims.

## Offer

In 5-10 delivery days, SEIS reviews one bounded AI-assisted engineering
workflow and produces:

- readiness scorecard
- evidence matrix
- risk-tier map
- review-gate recommendations
- rollback/remediation plan
- prioritized action plan
- proof-asset capture plan

## What Is Included

- discovery with economic buyer, technical owner, and risk owner
- review of supplied workflow evidence
- mapping of AI-code admission, tests, review, release, and rollback
- written findings with evidence and limitations
- buyer handoff session

## What Is Excluded

- production code changes
- deployment authority
- secret handling
- compliance certification
- live provider setup
- guarantee of revenue, ROI, audit pass, or customer adoption

## Best Fit

The audit is best fit when AI-written or AI-assisted code is already in PRs,
release paths, internal tools, test generation, or refactoring workflows, and
leadership needs a trustworthy governance answer within 30-90 days.

## Not Fit

Not fit for teams seeking uncontrolled automation, cheap code review,
generic AI training, secret custody, or certification language without
evidence.
