# Shot Bible v1

## Target

The sample target is 8-12 seconds total duration.

## Shot Sequence

1. Opening field emergence establishes the controlled energy source.
2. A motivated transition carries the motion into a readable hero frame.
3. The hero frame holds long enough to feel intentional and portfolio-ready.
4. The final resolve exits cleanly without random stylization drift.

## Hero Frame

One strong hero frame is mandatory. It must read at thumbnail size and remain commercially usable.

## Transition

One motivated transition is mandatory. The transition must be driven by field logic, particle motion, or compositing continuity rather than arbitrary prompt changes.

## Boundary

This is a planning document only. No execution, no subprocess, no provider execution, and no tool launching are authorized here.
