# No Fake Success Policy

## Purpose

Prevent SEIS from claiming completion, market proof, customer adoption,
certification, or maturity stages without evidence.

## Scope

Applies to all docs, reports, demos, runtime outputs, proof assets, and
external communications.

## Non-goals

- This policy is not a marketing style guide.
- It does not prevent ambition or pre-structure.
- It does not authorize hiding failures.

## Operating Rules

- A template is a template.
- A scaffold is a scaffold.
- A policy is a policy.
- Internal evidence is internal evidence.
- A demo is a demo.
- Market proof requires a real external actor.
- Revenue requires payment or equivalent buyer commitment.
- Stage 16 remains a compass until evidence supports the claim.

## Failure Modes

- Internal readiness is described as external recognition.
- A generated report is treated as a buyer.
- A recommended wedge is described as validated.

## Upgrade Path

Add automated claim linting only after the stable claim vocabulary emerges.
