# SaaS Path

## Purpose

Define what evidence would be required before considering public SaaS.

## SaaS Evidence Gates

- repeated paid demand across similar segments
- repeated accepted delivery loops
- clear self-serve workflow
- low support burden
- security model designed
- customer data boundaries clear
- retention or repeat-use signal
- pricing validated beyond bespoke services
- no dependency on manual expert judgment for core value

## Current Status

`MARKET_PROOF_PENDING`.

The current repository must not claim SaaS readiness, launch, users,
revenue, retention, or adoption.

## SaaS Not-Now Reasons

- no paid market proof yet
- no repeated delivery evidence yet
- no internal workbench implementation yet
- no support/security model yet
- no proof of self-serve demand
