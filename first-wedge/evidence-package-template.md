## Required Sections

- workflow map
- supplied artifacts
- evidence exclusions
- risk-tier findings
- review-gate findings
- human approval points
- rollback/remediation readiness
- readiness scorecard
- prioritized action plan
- unresolved gaps
- buyer acceptance notes

## Status Rules

- `evidenced`: source artifact exists and was reviewed.
- `pending`: source is missing, insufficient, or not approved for review.
- `rejected`: claim is unsupported or contradicted.

## Proof Asset Boundary

The evidence package may produce proof assets only after redaction, buyer
approval, and limitation review. No proof asset may imply external audit,
certification, paid traction, ROI, or customer success beyond the actual
record.
