# User Journeys

## Purpose

Define internal operator journeys for the future workbench.

## Journey 1 - Qualify A Buyer

1. Open target record.
2. Capture buyer, budget source, trigger, trust gap, and evidence access.
3. Apply rejection rules.
4. Create qualified next step or close/loss record.

## Journey 2 - Start A Delivery Loop

1. Create client workspace.
2. Confirm scope, risk owner, evidence access, and acceptance.
3. Generate intake package.
4. Require human approval before delivery state becomes active.

## Journey 3 - Review Evidence

1. Add evidence item.
2. Link it to a claim.
3. Assign risk tier.
4. Mark evidenced, pending, rejected, or needs review.
5. Route material claims to auditor.

## Journey 4 - Close Delivery

1. Assemble handoff package.
2. Check gaps and failures.
3. Record buyer acceptance or incomplete status.
4. Capture asset conversion decisions.
5. Update validation and asset ledgers.

## Journey 5 - Audit Claims

1. View claims by risk tier.
2. Compare evidence and limitation.
3. Downgrade unsupported claims.
4. Require human approval for external wording.

## Boundary

These are product journeys, not implementation proof. They remain
`APP_IMPLEMENTATION_PENDING`.
