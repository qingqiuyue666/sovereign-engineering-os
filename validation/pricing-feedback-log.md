# Pricing Feedback Log

## Purpose

Capture real price reactions without pretending pricing is validated before
buyers respond.

## Log

| Date | Buyer Role | Segment | Scope | Price Discussed | Reaction | Budget Source | Outcome | Follow-Up |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  |  |  |  |  | accepted / high / low / unclear / no budget / no decision |  |  |  |

## Required Notes

- What price level was discussed?
- Who controls the budget?
- What budget source was named?
- Was the objection price, value, timing, authority, or scope?
- Did the buyer compare to another alternative?
- Was a smaller scope preferable?

## Interpretation Rules

- A price mentioned by SEIS is not validated pricing.
- A buyer saying "sounds reasonable" is not paid signal.
- A discount request is not a loss until outcome is known.
- A no-budget response may indicate wrong buyer or weak trigger, not only
  wrong price.

## Update Rules

Update `first-wedge/pricing-ladder.md` only after repeated evidence or a
clear paid/won/lost outcome.
