# Creative Asset Review Rubric v1

## Purpose

This rubric gives the private operator a deterministic review surface for the first creative sample.

## Scoring Categories

- cinematic_quality
- motion_coherence
- lighting_consistency
- compositing_believability
- artifact_control
- ai_overuse_control
- commercial_usability

## AI Overuse Control

AI overuse control checks whether stylization supports the shot instead of becoming the shot. Reject obvious prompt-only aesthetics, texture mush, warped detail, repeated uncanny patterns, temporal flicker, and unmotivated style drift.

## Commercial Usability

Commercial usability checks whether the sample can function as a portfolio-ready proof: readable hero frame, clear motion, clean finish, no brand confusion, no unsafe claims, no obvious artifacts, and platform-ready deliverables.

## Review Decision Options

- pass
- needs_rework
- reject

## Rejection Rules

Reject if the sample loses the hero frame, fails motion coherence, shows cheap AI artifacts, lacks motivated transition, fails export requirements, or implies unauthorized execution.

## Rollback Plan

Rollback the rubric, deterministic builder, and tests together. Review rollback does not modify media or launch tools.
