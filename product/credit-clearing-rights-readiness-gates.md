# Credit Clearing Rights Readiness Gates

## Purpose

Define evidence required before using credit, clearing, or rights language.

## Gates

- contribution tracking exists across real deliveries
- asset rights and permissions are recorded
- buyer/client usage rights are clear
- reusable templates and standards have provenance
- value contribution can be attributed
- dispute process exists
- no confidential material is reused without approval

## Current Boundary

Credit, clearing, and rights remain placeholders. No credit ledger,
clearing system, or rights market is implemented.
