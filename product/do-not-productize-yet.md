# Do Not Productize Yet

## Purpose

Record what must not be built or claimed before evidence exists.

## Do Not Build Yet

- public SaaS
- certification portal
- template marketplace
- credit ledger runtime
- rights or clearing system
- capital allocation dashboard as an operating product
- live provider orchestration
- broad automation runtime
- external API connector platform

## Do Not Claim Yet

- product-market fit
- SaaS readiness
- certified standard
- protocol adoption
- marketplace demand
- credit or clearing validity
- capital allocation proof
- Stage 9-16 completion

## Next Real Action

Run validation, close or reject a paid audit, execute the delivery loop, and
populate asset records before revisiting productization.
