# Pricing Ladder

## Purpose

Define offer levels and price logic for the readiness audit while making
clear that real willingness to pay is still unvalidated.

## Price Ladder

| Level | Indicative Price | Fit | Scope | Exclusions |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| Discovery Diagnostic | USD 0-500 | Early validation or warm referral where proof is still weak. | One call, trigger map, fit/no-fit note. | No readiness claim, no report, no implementation. |
| Entry Readiness Audit | USD 3,000-7,500 | Small team with one workflow and named risk owner. | 5-10 days, scorecard, evidence matrix, risk tiers, gates, rollback plan. | No production changes, no certification, no secret custody. |
| Expanded Governance Audit | USD 10,000-20,000 | Multiple teams or multiple workflow paths. | Entry audit plus stakeholder interviews, workflow comparison, roadmap. | No broad transformation or ongoing management. |
| Implementation Support Add-On | USD 5,000-15,000 | Buyer accepts audit and wants help applying controls. | Templates, review-gate rollout support, evidence capture process. | No deployment authority unless separately approved. |
| Quarterly Governance Review | USD 3,000-10,000 per quarter | Team wants ongoing evidence hygiene. | Review updates, scorecard refresh, objection/risk register. | No outsourced ownership of production risk. |

## Pricing Rules

- Quote only after buyer, budget source, trigger, evidence access, and
  acceptance criteria are known.
- Increase price when scope includes multiple workflows, external-review
  pressure, compressed timeline, or higher evidence complexity.
- Decrease scope before decreasing price.
- Record every quote, objection, discount request, win, loss, and no-decision.
- Do not call any price validated until a buyer accepts or rejects it in a
  real sales context.

## Minimum Paid Signal

A credible paid signal requires at least one of:

- signed paid audit scope
- paid pilot
- written approval for paid work
- equivalent high-commitment commitment with named buyer, timeline, and
  evidence access

Interest, praise, free advice, or a friendly call is not paid signal.
