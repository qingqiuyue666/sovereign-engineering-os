Capture the minimum facts needed before starting the first audit loop.

## Required Before Start

- buyer sponsor named
- risk owner named
- workflow bounded
- evidence access path defined
- high-risk exclusions accepted
- approval points known

## Stop Conditions

- no risk owner
- no evidence access
- buyer expects certification or production authority
- secret handling is required for initial audit
- acceptance cannot be defined
