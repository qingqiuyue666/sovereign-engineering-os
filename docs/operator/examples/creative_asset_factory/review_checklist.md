- Confirm the target remains 8-12 seconds.
- Confirm one motivated transition is explicit.
- Confirm one strong hero frame is explicit.
- Confirm artifact control language is explicit.
- Confirm commercial usability is explicit.

## Boundary Review

- Confirm no Houdini execution is implied.
- Confirm no ComfyUI execution is implied.
- Confirm no DaVinci execution is implied.
- Confirm no provider execution is implied.

Pass only if the pack remains planning-only and commercially usable.
