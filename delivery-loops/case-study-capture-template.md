## Required Before Use

- buyer permission
- redaction
- limitation statement
- evidence source
- no inflated claims

## Asset Registry Update

After review, update the relevant registry:

- `assets/case-library.md`
- `assets/failure-library.md`
- `assets/customer-problem-library.md`
- `assets/objection-library.md`
- `assets/template-registry.md`
- `assets/delivery-trace-registry.md`

## Boundary

No case study exists until a real delivery and permission record exist.
