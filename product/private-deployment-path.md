# Private Deployment Path

## Purpose

Define when SEIS could become a private internal deployment for one client
or operator.

## Evidence Required

- at least two accepted delivery loops in comparable workflows
- repeated evidence package structure
- buyer asks for ongoing internal use
- security and permission boundaries designed
- support owner identified
- data retention and deletion policy defined
- no secret custody by default

## Candidate Scope

- internal evidence and claim tracker
- approval queue
- delivery loop dashboard
- asset conversion workflow
- reporting export

## Not Required

- public SaaS
- marketplace
- payment system
- generalized automation

## Stop Conditions

Do not deploy privately if the buyer needs production control, secret
custody, unsupported compliance claims, or broad autonomous execution.
