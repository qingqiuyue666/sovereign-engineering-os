# Discovery Script V1

Status label: `DISCOVERY_SCRIPT_READY`

Repository status: `SEIS_REAL_WORLD_VALIDATION_READY`

Real-world status: `HUMAN_ACTION_REQUIRED`

## Purpose

Turn a real conversation into buyer, budget, trigger, workflow, risk, and
evidence records without claiming validation before evidence exists.

## Opening Frame

Use this frame:

"I am testing whether one repeated workflow can be made safer and less manual
without risky automatic actions. I am not asking for credentials or production
access. I want to understand one workflow, where human approval is needed, what
evidence would prove improvement, and whether a bounded delivery is worth
scoping."

## Current Workflow

- What workflow do you repeat every day, week, or month?
- Who performs it today?
- What starts the workflow?
- What inputs are used?
- What output must be produced?
- What makes the workflow annoying, slow, or risky?
- What happens if the workflow is skipped or wrong?

## Frequency

- How often does this happen?
- How long does each run take?
- Does volume spike by season, customer, release, or campaign?
- How many people are involved?

## Time Cost

- How many hours are spent per week or month?
- Which steps are pure repetition?
- Which steps require judgment?
- Which steps create rework?

## Error Cost

- What mistakes happen most often?
- What is the cost of a mistake?
- Who notices errors?
- How are errors corrected?
- Are there examples that can be summarized safely?

## Approval Points

- Which steps require human review?
- Who approves the final output?
- What must never happen automatically?
- What would require manager approval?

## Tools Used

- What tools or files are involved?
- Are there spreadsheets, docs, tickets, forms, folders, messages, or apps?
- Can sanitized examples be shared?
- Are exports allowed?

## Data Sensitivity

- Does the workflow include personal data, customer data, financial data, HR
  data, regulated data, or secrets?
- What should not be stored in the repository?
- What must be redacted before review?
- Are there data retention rules?

## Account / Platform Risk

- Does the workflow touch external accounts?
- Could automation violate platform rules?
- Are payments, posts, messages, account settings, or customer promises
  involved?
- What must remain manual?

## Workflow Owner

- Who owns the workflow?
- Who is accountable when it fails?
- Who can approve a change?
- Who must review the output?

## Change Approver

- Who can approve a small process change?
- Who can approve paid work?
- Who can approve evidence access?
- Who can approve a maintenance relationship?

## Budget Path

- Is there budget for reducing this manual work?
- Which budget would pay for it?
- What amount can be approved without escalation?
- Who signs off?

## Urgency Trigger

- Why solve this now?
- Is there a deadline, audit, campaign, growth pressure, error trend, backlog,
  hiring gap, or customer issue?
- What happens if nothing changes for 30 days?

## Evidence Access

- What artifacts can show the current state?
- What artifacts can show improvement?
- Can before/after examples be redacted?
- Can time saved or errors reduced be estimated honestly?

## Success Criteria

- What would make this worth paying for?
- What output would you actually use?
- What must be included in the handoff?
- What evidence would make the result credible?

## Failure Criteria

- What would make the work useless?
- What would make it unsafe?
- What would make you reject the handoff?
- What would count as incomplete delivery?

## Maintenance Expectation

- Would the workflow need periodic updates?
- Who would maintain it?
- What would break it?
- Would monthly support be useful if the workflow is used?

## Close

Summarize:

- workflow
- pain
- frequency
- time/error cost
- risk boundary
- owner
- approver
- budget path
- trigger
- evidence access
- proposed next step
- explicit non-claims

Do not claim delivery readiness unless scope, evidence access, review owner,
and risk boundary are accepted.
