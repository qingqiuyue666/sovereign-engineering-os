## Failure Types

- Evidence failure: source artifact missing, weak, contradictory, or not
  approved for review.
- Scope failure: buyer expects work outside the agreed audit.
- Approval failure: required human approval is missing.
- Claim failure: wording exceeds evidence.
- Delivery failure: deliverable misses acceptance criteria.
- Buyer-fit failure: prospect should have been rejected.
- Pricing failure: price, value, or budget path is unsupported.

## Operating Rules

- Record failures even when the delivery is otherwise accepted.
- A failure record is not a marketing asset.
- Use failures to update rejection rules, review gates, scope, pricing, and
  templates.

## Boundary

Failure capture does not close the failure. Closure requires evidence of
mitigation, accepted risk, rescope, or rejection.
