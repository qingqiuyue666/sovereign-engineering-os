## Required Fields Before Outreach

- role
- likely trigger
- budget source hypothesis
- trust gap hypothesis
- channel
- reason they might care now

## Target Quality Rules

- Do not add generic AI enthusiasts without a production workflow.
- Do not add people who cannot influence budget or risk ownership unless
  they can introduce the buyer.
- Prefer named triggers over broad market categories.
- Record why a target is likely wrong-fit when uncertain.

## Evidence Status

The target list is a hypothesis file. It is not discovery, commitment, paid
signal, or market proof.
