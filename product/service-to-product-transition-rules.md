# Service To Product Transition Rules

## Purpose

Define when to keep SEIS as service and when to productize a repeated part.

## Keep As Service When

- buyer triggers vary widely
- evidence access is bespoke
- expert judgment drives the value
- risk ownership is unclear
- delivery pattern has fewer than two accepted repeats
- support/security requirements are unknown

## Productize When

- the same workflow repeats
- templates are reused with little modification
- acceptance criteria stabilize
- objections and pricing patterns repeat
- buyers ask for persistent internal use
- support boundaries are understood

## Transition Rule

Productize the narrowest repeated component first. Do not productize the
whole SEIS system before repeated validated workflow patterns exist.
