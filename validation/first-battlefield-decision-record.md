## Decision

Selected first real-world validation battlefield:

`Trusted AI Workflow Transformation`

This decision chooses the first validation campaign, not the final company
strategy and not a validated market claim.

## Alternatives Rejected Or Deferred

| Alternative | Decision | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `AI Engineering Production Governance` | Defer to secondary/high-trust wedge | Strong strategic fit in the repository, but higher buyer-access threshold and more trust needed before first validation. |
| Generic AI automation service | Reject | Too broad, commodity-prone, and likely to encourage unsafe automation claims. |
| Public App or SaaS path | Reject for now | Productization would avoid market contact and lacks repeated workflow evidence. |
| Protocol, credit, clearing, rights, or capital layer | Reject for now | These require repeated real delivery and external adoption evidence. |

## Why Selected

`Trusted AI Workflow Transformation` is selected because it can be tested with
real operators through concrete workflows:

- spreadsheet cleanup
- report generation
- file/archive handoff
- content prep
- customer-data cleanup
- recruiting/application review prep
- simple admin and operations work

It has a lower access threshold than engineering governance while still
testing the core SEIS thesis: real workflows require human approval, evidence,
SOPs, failure handling, and reusable assets.

## Why Not Product, App, Or SaaS Now

The repository already says the App is the shell, not the moat. Product or
SaaS implementation now would create build activity before buyer evidence.

No current repository evidence proves:

- repeated buyer demand
- validated pricing
- accepted scope
- repeated delivery
- app workflow demand
- external adoption

Therefore the correct next step is human outreach and discovery.

## What Would Falsify The Battlefield

The battlefield is falsified or paused if:

- target operators do not recognize the pain
- workflows are too low-value to pay for
- buyers only want full automation without review
- evidence access is consistently blocked
- delivery cannot be bounded safely
- repeated objections show the offer is generic or not urgent
- the first 10 relevant outreach attempts produce no actionable discovery
