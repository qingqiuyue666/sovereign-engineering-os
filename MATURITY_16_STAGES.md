# 16-Stage Maturity Model

## Purpose

Provide the long-term maturity compass for SEIS. These stages are not
folders and not a task checklist. They define the path from one useful
delivery to institution-level infrastructure.

## Scope

Applies to strategy, evidence claims, market execution, delivery standards,
proof assets, ecosystem development, protocol design, credit, rights,
clearing, and capital allocation.

## Non-goals

- Do not claim a stage because documentation exists.
- Do not implement these stages as 16 separate directories.
- Do not treat Stage 16 as complete in the current repository.

## Stage Definitions

| Stage | Name | Evidence Required Before Claim |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Can Deliver Once | One useful delivery with acceptance criteria and evidence package. |
| 2 | Someone Is Willing To Look | A real person inspects the result or asks for the proof/demo. |
| 3 | Someone Is Willing To Pay | Payment, signed pilot, purchase order, or equivalent buyer commitment. |
| 4 | Repurchase / Referral | Follow-up purchase, referral, renewal, or expanded request. |
| 5 | Stable Distribution Entry | Repeatable channel that reaches the right buyer slice. |
| 6 | Standardized Delivery System | SOPs, templates, review gates, proof, and risk boundaries. |
| 7 | Data / Asset Compounding Flywheel | Delivery creates reusable workflows, cases, failures, pricing, objections, benchmarks, and ROI records. |
| 8 | Default Solution In A Narrow Slice | The system becomes preferred for a specific slice. |
| 9 | Ecosystem Control Layer | Partners, clients, contributors, or operators work around the system's standards. |
| 10 | Single-Slice Institutional / Infrastructure Layer | Trusted infrastructure or de facto standard in one slice. |
| 11 | Multi-Slice Trusted Delivery Layer | The same trusted delivery mechanism works across slices. |
| 12 | Cross-Industry AI Work / Transaction Protocol Layer | Shared protocols, evidence, and acceptance rules enable transactions across actors. |
| 13 | Intelligent Economic Resource Allocation Layer | Tasks, agents, humans, tools, workflows, risk, and price are allocated across a network. |
| 14 | Credit / Clearing / Rights Layer | Contribution, responsibility, credit, trust, ownership, permissions, and settlement are tracked. |
| 15 | Cross-Market Capital Allocation / Industry-Shaping Layer | Real delivery, risk, ROI, and market data guide investment and expansion. |
| 16 | Civilization-Level Knowledge / Institution / Productivity Operating System | World change, production, trust, assets, resources, and institutions are continuously organized. |

## Operating Rules

- Each stage claim must link to a proof record in `reports/`, `proof/`,
  `assets/`, or a verified external evidence packet.
- A higher-stage design may exist before a lower-stage proof exists, but it
  must be labeled pre-structure or scaffold.
- Advancement requires evidence of buyer behavior, delivery reliability,
  distribution repeatability, and asset compounding.

## Failure Modes

- Calling a scaffold a stage completion.
- Treating an internal benchmark as external market proof.
- Promoting ecosystem or credit language before any delivery standard is
  adopted by others.

## Upgrade Path

Use `VALIDATION_REPORT.md` and `reports/gap-list.md` to keep stage claims
below evidence. Add a stage ledger only after real transactions begin.
