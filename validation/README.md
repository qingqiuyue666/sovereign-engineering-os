# Real-World Validation Kit

## Purpose

Prepare SEIS to validate the first wedge with real buyers without claiming
validation before evidence exists.

## What Counts As Discovery

Discovery means a real target or buyer discusses their current workflow,
budget path, buying trigger, trust gap, evidence access, objections, and
decision process. A generic conversation about AI does not count.

## What Counts As Commitment

Commitment means the buyer agrees to a next action with owner, date, scope,
and evidence access. Strong commitment includes paid scope, signed pilot, or
equivalent high-commitment approval.

## What Counts As Rejection

Rejection means the buyer or SEIS says no after a specific offer, scope,
price, evidence requirement, or safety boundary is discussed.

## What Counts As Paid Signal

Paid signal requires payment, signed paid scope, approved paid pilot, or an
equivalent high-commitment record with named buyer, timeline, and evidence
access.

## What Does Not Count

- praise
- friendly interest
- internal enthusiasm
- unpaid advice
- social media engagement
- generic AI curiosity
- a meeting with no next step
- repository scaffold
- demo without buyer commitment

## Evidence Needed Before Stronger Claims

Before claiming market proof, SEIS needs real buyer records showing segment,
role, trigger, budget, evidence access, objection, price response, outcome,
and acceptance or rejection. Stronger claims require repeated records, not a
single anecdote.
