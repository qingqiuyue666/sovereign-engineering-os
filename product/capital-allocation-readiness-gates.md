# Capital Allocation Readiness Gates

## Purpose

Define what evidence is required before SEIS can support capital allocation
language.

## Gates

- repeated paid delivery outcomes
- pricing and margin history
- ROI or risk-reduction evidence
- segment-level demand evidence
- standardization candidates with acceptance records
- clear control-point advantage
- downside and failure data

## Current Boundary

Capital allocation remains analysis only. No investment product, fund,
allocation engine, or financial recommendation is created.
