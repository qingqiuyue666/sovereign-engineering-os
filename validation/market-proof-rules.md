# Market Proof Rules

## Purpose

Prevent discovery activity from being inflated into market validation.

## Claim Ladder

| Claim Level | Allowed Evidence | Forbidden Upgrade |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hypothesis | Internal reasoning, target list, offer draft. | Cannot claim buyer demand. |
| Discovery signal | Real conversation with role, trigger, objection, and next step. | Cannot claim paid traction. |
| Paid signal | Paid scope or equivalent high-commitment record. | Cannot claim repeatable market proof. |
| Delivery proof | Accepted delivery package and buyer acceptance. | Cannot claim product readiness. |
| Repeatable proof | Multiple paid, accepted deliveries in a similar segment. | Cannot claim certification/protocol/credit maturity. |

## Market Proof Requires

- repeated buyer conversations in the target segment
- named buyer roles and budget paths
- recorded triggers
- price response
- win/loss/no-decision data
- evidence-access feasibility
- at least one paid or equivalent high-commitment signal before paid-demand
  language

## Forbidden Claims Without Evidence

- paid customer signal
- customer adoption
- revenue
- external validation
- proven ROI
- certification
- product-market fit
- SaaS readiness
- Stage 9-16 completion

## Review Cadence

Review market proof after the first 10 conversations, after the first paid
signal, and after each accepted delivery. Keep the wedge, revise it, or kill
it based on recorded evidence.
