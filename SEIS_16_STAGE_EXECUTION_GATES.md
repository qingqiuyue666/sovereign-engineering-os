# SEIS 16-Stage Execution Gates

## Purpose

Define the 16-stage path from doctrine to capital allocation while keeping
repository completion separate from real-world completion.

No stage may upgrade because a document exists. Repository work can prepare
gates, templates, audits, and specs. Real-world completion requires evidence
from buyers, delivery, adoption, external parties, transaction history, or
capital outcomes as applicable.

Real-World Validation | Force contact with real buyers and workflows. | Outreach, discovery, scoring, close/loss, objection, pricing, paid-signal, and market-proof templates. | Real conversations and outcomes are recorded. | Treating outreach plans as outreach evidence or curiosity as market proof. | Execute the validation command layer. | Stop building new strategy layers and contact real targets. | Repository: `EXECUTION_KIT_READY`; Real-world: `HUMAN_ACTION_REQUIRED`. |
| 8. First Delivery Loop | Complete the first real delivery loop. | Intake, current-state map, risk map, governance gap template, recommendation report, evidence matrix, handoff, case capture. | A real workflow is diagnosed, transformed, delivered, reviewed, and recorded. | Treating a delivery-loop template as a case study or ROI proof. | Attempt one bounded delivery after buyer acceptance. | Stop if real workflow access or acceptance evidence is missing. | Repository: `EXECUTION_KIT_READY`; Real-world: `REAL_DELIVERY_PENDING`. |
| 9. Asset Compounding | Turn delivery into reusable advantage. | Asset conversion rules, registries, case library, failure library, SOP/template/pricing/objection/tool records. | Real delivery artifacts are converted into reusable assets. | Treating empty registries or hypothetical assets as compounding proof. | Convert actual delivery outputs into assets with source/date/limitation. | Stop if the asset lacks evidence or reuse condition. | Repository: `EXECUTION_KIT_READY`; Real-world: `EVIDENCE_PENDING`. |
| 10. | 11. Private Deployment | Package repeatable workflows for selected customers. | Private deployment path, maintenance gates, customer-specific boundary rules. | A customer-specific deployment is accepted and maintained. | Treating internal docs or workbench spec as private deployment. | Revisit after repeated delivery evidence and customer maintenance demand. | Stop if there is no accepted customer-specific workflow. | Repository: `SCAFFOLD_COMPLETE`; Real-world: `REAL_DELIVERY_PENDING`. |
| 12. Productization | Move from service to product only when repeated evidence exists. | Productization roadmap, service-to-product rules, SaaS not-now gates. | Repeated validated patterns justify product implementation. | Building product/SaaS to avoid service validation. | Standardize only repeated patterns. | Stop if evidence is one-off, weak, or speculative. | Repository: `SCAFFOLD_COMPLETE`; Real-world: `EVIDENCE_PENDING`. |
| 13. Marketplace / Ecosystem | Enable third-party or internal templates/assets only after standards emerge. | Marketplace readiness gates, partner/ecosystem placeholder rules. | Multiple validated assets are reused across independent contexts. | Calling asset folders a marketplace or ecosystem traction. | Track reuse after real asset conversion. | Stop if assets have no independent reuse evidence. | Repository: `SCAFFOLD_COMPLETE`; Real-world: `EXTERNAL_VALIDATION_PENDING`. |
| 14. Protocol / Rights Layer | Define standards, rights, and interoperability only after repeated evidence. | Protocol and rights readiness gates, adoption requirements, authority boundaries. | External parties adopt or depend on the standard. | Treating readiness gates as protocol adoption or rights operation. | Document adoption requirements only; do not implement rights systems. | Stop until external adoption evidence exists. | Repository: `SCAFFOLD_COMPLETE`; Real-world: `EXTERNAL_VALIDATION_PENDING`. |
| 15. Credit / Clearing Layer | Define credit/clearing logic around execution trust only after reliable evidence. | Credit/clearing readiness gates, placeholder schemas, trust-score evidence requirements. | Real transaction histories support trust scoring or clearing behavior. | Treating placeholder ledgers as credit or clearing systems. | Keep credit/clearing as future gates. | Stop until repeated real transaction histories exist. | Repository: `SCAFFOLD_COMPLETE`; Real-world: `CAPITAL_PROOF_PENDING`. |
| 16. Capital Allocation Layer | Use evidence and compounding assets to inform capital/resource allocation. | Capital allocation readiness gates and evidence requirements. | Real revenue, delivery history, risk record, and asset performance justify capital allocation. | Treating strategic ambition or internal dashboards as capital allocation. | Record evidence requirements; do not allocate capital. | Stop until real capital proof exists. | Repository: `SCAFFOLD_COMPLETE`; Real-world: `CAPITAL_PROOF_PENDING`. |

## Cross-Stage Upgrade Rules

- Repository completion requires files, audits, checks, and reviewable diffs.
- Real-world completion requires evidence with source, date, actor, claim,
  and limitation.
- Human action is required whenever evidence must come from buyers,
  customers, external parties, delivery outcomes, payment behavior, or
  capital decisions.
- `APP_SPEC_READY` is not app implementation.
- `SCAFFOLD_COMPLETE` is not market proof.
- `EXECUTION_KIT_READY` is not real delivery.
- `STRATEGIC_OS_READY` is not Stage 16 maturity.
