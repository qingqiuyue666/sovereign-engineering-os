# DaVinci Finishing Checklist v1

## Purpose

This checklist defines finishing review expectations for the first private 8-12 second cinematic VFX/AI/3D hybrid sample.

## No DaVinci Execution

No DaVinci execution is performed. This checklist does not launch DaVinci Resolve, does not open local GUI tools, does not render video, and does not execute external actions.

## Color Pipeline

- Establish a neutral working transform before look decisions.
- Preserve highlight detail unless bloom is deliberately motivated.
- Keep subject separation readable through the hero frame.

## Contrast Targets

- Strong silhouette in the hero frame.
- Controlled black floor without crushed field detail.
- Highlight energy that supports cinematic depth rather than noisy clipping.

## Highlight Policy

Highlights must feel motivated by field energy or particle emission. Reject random bloom, harsh clipping, and smeared AI halos.

## Subject Separation

The sample must remain readable on mobile and desktop review. Depth, lighting, contrast, and emission should separate the hero element from background energy.

## Export Formats

- Review master.
- Social vertical crop plan.
- Social square crop plan.
- Thumbnail frame.

## Rejection Rules

Reject if the finish hides AI artifacts rather than fixing them, damages motion coherence, loses the hero frame, breaks duration, or implies unauthorized external execution.

## Rollback Plan

Rollback is a checklist revision only. Do not launch DaVinci Resolve or modify media through this document.
