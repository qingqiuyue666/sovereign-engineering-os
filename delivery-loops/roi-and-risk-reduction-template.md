# ROI And Risk Reduction Template

## Purpose

Capture a bounded ROI and risk-reduction hypothesis without claiming proven
ROI before evidence exists.

## ROI Boundary

This template may state hypotheses such as reduced review ambiguity,
faster readiness decisions, fewer unsupported claims, or clearer rollback
ownership. It must not claim proven ROI until measured evidence exists.
