## Categories

- budget: price, approval, or funding path
- trigger: urgency is weak or absent
- trust: buyer doubts evidence, process, or value
- scope: buyer wants more or different work
- evidence: buyer cannot share artifacts
- timing: no near-term decision
- authority: contact cannot approve or influence
- safety: request violates boundaries

## Update Rules

- One objection does not rewrite the offer.
- Repeated objections should update outreach, service page, discovery, or
  pricing language.
- Safety objections should update rejection rules immediately if needed.
- Do not overcome an objection by inflating claims.
