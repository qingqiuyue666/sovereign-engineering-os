# Maintenance Options

## Purpose

Define post-audit options that can be offered without converting the entry
audit into uncontrolled ongoing execution.

## Options

| Option | Fit | Scope | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 30-Day Remediation Review | Buyer wants help prioritizing immediate fixes. | Review progress against action plan and update evidence matrix. | No production authority. |
| Quarterly Governance Review | Buyer wants recurring evidence hygiene. | Refresh scorecard, risks, review gates, and failure records. | Not outsourced ownership. |
| Review-Gate Rollout Support | Buyer accepted gates and wants adoption support. | Templates, training, checklist rollout, feedback capture. | No deployment or secret handling. |
| Evidence Package Refresh | Buyer needs updated internal/customer review packet. | Update sources, limitations, and readiness language. | No certification claim. |
| Implementation Support Scope | Buyer needs specific controls implemented. | Separate scope, price, risk tier, approvals, and acceptance. | Not included in entry audit. |

## Upgrade Rules

- Offer maintenance only after audit acceptance or clear incomplete status.
- Keep each option tied to evidence, not vague advisory hours.
- Require new approval for production-adjacent or external-claim work.
- Record all maintenance outcomes in asset and pricing histories.

## Do Not Offer Yet

- certification program
- public SaaS
- autonomous governance runtime
- credit, rights, clearing, or protocol implementation
- managed production authority
