# Eight Engines

## Purpose

Define the SEIS operating architecture. The master prompt listed ten
surfaces; this repository uses eight engines by consolidating adjacent
surfaces while retaining dedicated directories for operational material.

## Scope

Covers world intelligence, market structure, control/battlefield selection,
transaction design, proof/distribution, trusted delivery/brain governance,
asset compounding, and ecosystem/protocol/credit/capital pre-structure.

## Non-goals

- This is not a runtime implementation plan.
- This is not a claim that every engine has real-world traction.
- This does not remove existing kernel, governance, execution-plane, or
  creative-pipeline assets.

## Engine Map

| Engine | Directories | Control Question | Output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. World Intelligence | `world/` | What forces are changing budgets, trust, labor, regulation, platforms, compute, and institutions? | Macro force maps and pressure signals. |
| 2. Market Structure | `market/` | Which buyers have budgets, triggers, losses, channels, and trust thresholds? | Buyer/budget/trigger maps. |
| 3. Control Point and Battlefield | `control-points/`, `battlefield/` | Which narrow slice can become standards, protocols, assets, and defensible control? | Ranked wedges and kill zones. |
| 4. Transaction | `transaction/` | What can be sold, scoped, priced, accepted, rejected, and upgraded now? | Offers, pricing, screening, contracts, margin logic. |
| 5. Proof and Distribution | `proof/`, `distribution/` | How does every delivery become trust, proof, content, referrals, and channel learning? | Case studies, ROI, service pages, posts, demos. |
| 6. Trusted Delivery and Brain Governance | `trusted-delivery/`, `brain/` | How do AI, humans, tools, and local runtimes produce trusted work without fake authority? | SOPs, policies, review separation, model hierarchy. |
| 7. Asset Compounding | `assets/` | What reusable workflows, templates, failures, benchmarks, and traces accumulate? | Registries and compounding ledgers. |
| 8. Ecosystem / Protocol / Credit / Capital | `ecosystem/`, `protocol/`, `credit/`, `capital/` | What could become standards, partner networks, rights, clearing, and allocation signals later? | Pre-structure schemas and dashboards. |

## Operating Rules

- Engines must connect. A battlefield wedge must point to buyer budget,
  transaction structure, proof asset, delivery SOP, asset registry, and
  upward path.
- The architecture must reject feature-only work that lacks a control point
  or paid-signal path.
- Existing runtime and creative assets are integrated as trusted-delivery,
  proof, and asset-compounding evidence, not as the strategy itself.

## Failure Modes

- Separate directories drift into unconnected documents.
- Strategy bypasses transaction design.
- Transaction design bypasses trusted delivery and proof.
- Protocol or capital language appears before evidence supports it.

## Upgrade Path

After the first 90-day paid-signal test, attach evidence to each engine and
replace assumptions with buyer and delivery records.
