# Discovery Call Script

## Purpose

Qualify whether the readiness audit has a real buyer, real trigger, real
budget path, real evidence access, and a bounded acceptance path.

## Opening

Frame the call:

"I am testing fit for a bounded AI Production Governance Readiness Audit.
The audit reviews one AI-assisted engineering workflow and produces a
readiness scorecard, evidence matrix, risk-tier map, review gates, rollback
plan, and prioritized next actions. It does not require production access,
secret custody, or certification claims."

## Qualification Questions

### Current Workflow

- Where is AI-assisted code or AI-generated engineering work entering your
  process today?
- Is it used for production code, tests, refactors, internal tools, docs, or
  review support?
- What artifacts would show the workflow clearly?

### Buyer And Budget

- Who owns the risk if an AI-assisted change causes a production issue?
- Who would approve spend for reducing that risk?
- Which budget would this likely come from?
- Who else must agree that this is worth doing?

### Trigger

- Why look at this now rather than later?
- Is there a release, audit, customer review, incident, board concern, or AI
  policy rollout driving urgency?
- What happens if no governance change is made in the next 90 days?

### Trust Gap

- What would leadership need to trust before scaling AI-assisted coding?
- What evidence would be credible internally?
- What claims would be unsafe or premature?

### Evidence Access

- Can you share sanitized PRs, tickets, test output, review notes, release
  checklists, incidents, or workflow docs?
- Are there security or privacy limits that would block a useful audit?
- Who can approve evidence access?

### Acceptance

- What would make the audit clearly useful?
- What deliverable format would be easiest to act on?
- What decision should the audit support?
- What would count as a failed or incomplete audit?

## Close

Summarize:

- buyer
- budget source
- trigger
- trust gap
- evidence available
- proposed scope
- exclusions
- acceptance criteria
- next action

Do not quote or claim readiness until the scope, evidence, and approval path
are confirmed.

## Disqualify If

- no risk owner exists
- no trigger exists
- no evidence can be shared
- buyer wants uncontrolled automation or secret handling
- buyer expects certification or guaranteed ROI
