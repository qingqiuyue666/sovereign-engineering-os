# Risk Map Template

## Purpose

Turn the current-state map into a risk view that can drive governance
recommendations.

## Risk Table

| Risk | Tier | Workflow Step | Owner | Evidence | Impact | Recommended Control | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  | 0 / 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 |  |  |  |  |  | open / mitigated / accepted / pending |

## Required Risk Areas

- AI-code admission
- review sufficiency
- test evidence
- approval authority
- rollback readiness
- external claim risk
- secret/privacy exposure
- ownership ambiguity

## Approval

Tier 3 and Tier 4 risks require risk-owner approval before final readiness
language is used.
