# Codex Organizational Operating Layer

Layer id: `ORGANIZATIONAL_OPERATING_LAYER`

## Purpose

Route work to the right owner when Codex-only action is insufficient.

## Connected Loop

organizational operating -> reviewer/security/data/release/human responsibility queues

Queues are recorded in
`reports/checkpoints/continuous-maturity-iteration-queue-v1.md`, the scorecard,
and final reports.

## Evidence Gate

Each unresolved item records owner type: Codex-only, human reviewer, security
reviewer, data/license reviewer, production owner, CI/admin access, or external
benchmark access.

## Non-Claim Boundary

Queueing ownership does not mean the review or approval occurred.
