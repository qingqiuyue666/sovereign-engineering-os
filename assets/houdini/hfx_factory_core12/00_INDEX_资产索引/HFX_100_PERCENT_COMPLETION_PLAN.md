## True 100 Percent Definition
- All 12 Core12 assets are audited.
- At least HFX_008 is gold_complete under the HFX Gold Asset Standard.
- HFX_015 is production_candidate or better.
- Every Core12 asset has a HIP/HDA or an explicit rebuild plan.
- Every Core12 asset has a validation plan.
- Every Core12 asset has a preview/render proof plan.
- A shot-bound validation path exists.
- External asset decision remains deferred until internal closure is complete.
- No fake film-grade claims are made.
- No external raw assets are committed.

## Current Decision Boundary
This plan does not claim current 100% unless the HFX Core12 Completion Ledger supports it. The current closure branch records audit completion separately from asset completion and keeps final-pixel, render, and external-asset claims out of scope unless evidence gates exist.

## Required Closure Actions
- Promote every Core12 asset from candidate status to gold_complete or production_complete with validated evidence.
- Add missing rebuild plans, validation plans, preview proof plans, and shot-bound proof plans per asset.
- Preserve the no external raw asset boundary until license review and internal closure are complete.
